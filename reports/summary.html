Shot-making or shot selection? Separating the two in NBA shooting

An expected-make model validated on unseen seasons, then three questions for a front office: how much shooters differ at making shots, whether it lasts, and what teams control.

Andres Perez, M.S. Kinesiology (Biomechanics)Python · scikit-learn · statsmodelsCode: github.com/Andresperez397/nba-shot-making

Data

1,897,373 regular-season field-goal attempts from 2017-18 to 2025-26 (NBA Stats play-by-play). Inputs are shot location, shot type and game context. The model is tested on seven seasons it never trained on (1.47M shots).

Approach

  • Audit, then a frozen plan: fields that reveal the result (description text, assists, blocks, the shot row's own score) are excluded; the pre-shot score is rebuilt from earlier events.
  • Out-of-sample expected makes: each season is scored by a model trained only on earlier seasons.
  • Empirical-Bayes shrinkage for player shot-making; cluster bootstraps for every comparison.
  • A data break caught: the NBA moved rim-shot coordinates in 2017-18, so the window starts there.

Findings

  • Location and shot type predict makes; game context adds almost nothing. Gradient boosting beat a spline logistic model, and the model is well calibrated (slope 1.03).
  • Shooters differ by about 7 points per 100 shots in true shot-making. Jokić holds the six best seasons.
  • Diet is far steadier than making: split-half r is 0.93 for shot selection and 0.44 for shot-making.
  • Making is still a skill: shrunk shot-making predicts next season's better than raw numbers or assuming no skill (r = 0.55).
  • Defenses control the shots they allow (year-to-year r 0.77) far more than whether opponents make them (0.39).

Robustness and limitations

Results hold with location-only expected makes, which avoid scorer-assigned shot-type labels (the two shot-making estimates correlate 0.96), and with shot-making centered within each season. Public data have no defender positions, so part of measured shot-making is shot quality the data can't see, which may favor bigs and players who get open looks. Tips and putbacks have no charted location after 2020-21.
